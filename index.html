08 — Before you write

The facts, plainly.

Who are the classes for?

Adults, ages 18 to 40, who want a creative break from work life in Dubai. MayGrid teaches guitar and vocals.

What will we play?

Western, Bollywood and South Indian covers. Performances have included Malayalam folk rock, rock gigs and Hindi film songs.

When are classes, and what do they cost?

Every Sunday. Four classes a month, for AED 250.

Can I try a class before I join?

Yes. There is a free live trial class on Sunday, and a guitar is provided for each participant.

What happens besides the Sunday class?

Group jams, solo and duo practice, workshops, and an Annual Day concert where students receive certificates on stage.

Where is the studio?

Near Sharaf DG Metro station, Dubai. Message on WhatsApp for the exact meeting point — a street address is not published here.

How do I book?

WhatsApp +971 54 540 9151. MayGrid Music is on Instagram at @maygridmusic, and the instructor, Jaabi Baapu, is at @jaabi_baapu.

The trial is free. The guitar is provided.

A live class on Sunday, near Sharaf DG Metro. After that, four classes a month are AED 250.
